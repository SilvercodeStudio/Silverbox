# Settings

Open Settings with Command-Comma.

## General

The app icon, amber or green, which the Dock and the About window show and whose colour the app uses for highlights; the appearance, light or dark or matching the system (System Settings › Appearance); menu music on or off, its volume, and whether it keeps playing when another app is in front; the app that plays videos (the macOS default for MP4 files unless you choose one); whether to show the launch options dialog before each game; whether hidden games appear in the library.

## Library

The eXoDOS folder and what was found in it; the local data folder; where new games are installed; Rescan Library.

## Emulators

Where DOSBox Staging and DOSBox-X are installed, and which one to use by default. Then where 86Box is installed, for the eXoWin9x games that need it. Below them, RetroArch (with its cores folder), Dolphin, MAME and Mednafen for console, computer and arcade platforms, and a list of those platforms with the emulator and core each one uses.

## Display & Sound

The defaults for every launch: full screen or window, window size, aspect ratio correction, CRT shader and General MIDI rendering. The launch options dialog starts from these values.

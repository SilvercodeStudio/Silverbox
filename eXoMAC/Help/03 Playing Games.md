# Playing games

Select a game and press Play, press Command-Return, or double-click its box.

## Installing

The first time you play a game, eXo Client unpacks its archive into the local data folder (or the eXoDOS folder, see Settings). If the collection ships an update for the game, it is applied automatically. If you uninstalled the game before and kept a backup, you can restore your saved progress.

## The launch options dialog

Before a game starts, eXo Client shows its launch options:

- **Version** — some games come in several editions, for example CGA, EGA and VGA releases, or original and patched versions. This matches the menus eXoDOS shows in its console window.
- **Full screen** or a window, and the window size.
- **Aspect ratio correction** and the **CRT shader** (DOSBox Staging).
- **Emulator** — DOSBox Staging or DOSBox-X.

Turn on "Remember these choices for this game" to keep them for next time. To skip the dialog, turn off "Show launch options before each game" in Settings › General; the Configure button and Edit Game Info keep per-game choices reachable.

## Uninstalling

Choose Uninstall from the game's context menu. eXo Client offers to back up your saved games and any changed files first. The backup is a small archive in the local data folder's `Saves` folder, and it is offered for restore the next time you install the game.

## Extras and manuals

The inspector lists the game's manual and extras: reference cards, maps, cluebooks and guide books. Click one for Quick Look, or use the context menu to open it in another app.

## Menu music

Many games have a music track that plays while you browse. Use the bar at the bottom of the window to pause it or change the volume, or turn it off in Settings › General.
